## Learned Radial Readout

Radial fits use sampled learned gates from each trained model. Ring/bond use the
axis gate as the analytic-compatible signed gate when axes are present. Charge
uses the displacement gate.

### Ring

- Chosen signed gate powers vary and the `intensity * r^-3` comparator is weak:
  C 0.087, CA 0.0069, HA 0.0045, HN 0.427, N 0.219, O 0.0269.
- Evidence status: weak/partial radial match, not a robust Pople-like readout in
  this broad-backbone model.

### Bond

- The category-specific `r^-3` comparator is strong for several heavy-atom and
  amide strata: C 0.979, O 0.967, HN 0.955, N 0.885.
- CA/HA/HA2/HA3 are weaker: 0.212, 0.243, 0.164, 0.178.
- Evidence status: the strongest radial evidence is bond-like, but it is uneven
  across target strata.

### Charge

- The corrected T2/EFG radial-only comparator is `q * r^-3`, not `q * r^-2`.
- N is the strongest match: `gate_vs_q_r^-3` R2 0.982.
- Other strata are mixed: O 0.390, CA 0.369, HA3 0.332, C 0.282, HA2 0.264,
  HA 0.238, HN 0.033.
- Evidence status: mixed. One stratum strongly matches the T2/EFG radial form,
  but the pooled/readout evidence is not uniformly Buckingham-like.

## Caveats

- McConnell/ring current were not edited in the capstone fix. F1 is resolved in
  favor of the canonical McConnell `K * chi` traceless-chi PCS convention; see
  `/shared/2026Thesis/nmr-shielding/GEOMETRIC_KERNEL_CATALOGUE.md`. The
  remaining documentation obligation is to disclose that PCS convention, not to
  change the implementation.
- T2 variance shares are pooled-Frobenius shares over emitted rank-2 components.
  They are not scalar variance shares for a single tensor component.
- The DFT substrate is r2SCAN/def2-SVP with CPCM(Water). Electrostatic kernels
  based on fixed-charge or APBS features should be read with that solvation and
  basis-set mismatch in mind.
- Literature-coefficient-fixed prediction remains a substrate gap for several
  backbone mechanisms. The corrected evidence reads emitted columns and avoids
  rebuilding fixed physics in Python.
