Notation and proof status
-------------------------
R is any orthogonal matrix; d=det(R); t is translation.  P means polar
v'=Rv; A means axial a'=dRv; X means affine position p'=Rp+t; T means full
even-rank-2 T'=RTR^T; T2 means the native five-component projection obtained
by reconstructing T, applying RTR^T and decomposing again; PS means
pseudoscalar s'=d s.  "proper-only" is an SO(3) contract, not an O(3) claim.
"NC" means orientation dependent but not a closed homogeneous representation.

SZ(T0/T1) means symmetric-traceless physics makes PackFull9 T0 and Cartesian
Levi-Civita-dual T1 structural zeros.  "physical zero" is a real empty sum;
"legacy zero" is an unavailable/physical-zero ambiguity.  NaN/mask names are
part of the contract, not numerical cleanup.

Focused proof IDs are those defined with seeds/tolerances in
DIRECTIONAL_OUTPUT_INVENTORY.txt.  The focused C++ covariance tests listed
below passed in this worktree before this report was written.  NPY-E2E means
NpyLocalGeometryAcceptance.StaticAndTrajectoryProductionPaths: one static
OperationRunner extraction, a fresh proper rigid-transform rerun, and one real
FrameNpyEmitter frame, with every emitted file opened using
numpy.load(..., allow_pickle=False).  Its final rebuilt rerun passed after the
last metadata/test edits.  "owner-NPY" means the named owner test itself read
the serialized payload.  H5-PASS means the transformed owner result was
finalized, written and read at the H5 boundary.  GAP means the exact serialized
name/path does not yet have the required transformed-input boundary proof.

The static reader gate is name-complete: every one of the 178 exact rows below
is opened with numpy.load(..., allow_pickle=False), either in NPY-E2E when the
ordinary production extraction emits it or in the named optional/external
owner proof.  The owner-specific closure includes AIMNet2, APBS and its
Coulomb aliases, Coulomb/MOPAC-Coulomb, ORCA/mutation, Gromacs, Larsen and
MOPAC parser outputs; it does not substitute for their separate
physics oracles.

The static NPY machine contract includes metadata correction 6bfe6c7: every
nonhomogeneous row enumerated below is parity=mixed, false global irreps were
removed from ring_contributions.npy and cb_residual_vector.npy, and
dssp_ss8.npy is explicitly even with a physical O(3)-invariant continuum law;
the later DSSP owner correction records that 0.001-A temporary-PDB rounding
can cross a serialized decision boundary.  The exact mixed-stem set and
contract strings are pinned by python/tests/test_topology_sidecar.py.

Welford rule used below
-----------------------
For every directional Welford group, only the assembled mean is a vector/T2.
Per-component m2/std/min/max and the frames selected by component extrema are
orientation-dependent NC statistics.  They must not inherit the mean's irrep.
When a per-row sample count is zero, group metadata says mean/m2/std=NaN,
min=+inf, max=-inf, and min/max_frame=0 are invalid sentinels; the corresponding
n_frames_per_atom gates validity (the AIMNet gradient owner names this count
n_per_atom).  All exact datasets below were written/read
by DCT-SA, DCT-KW or DCT-WW, or by the owner metadata/zero-count tests as stated.
